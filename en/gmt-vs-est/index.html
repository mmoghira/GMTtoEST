What is GMT?

GMT is the zero-offset reference used for global coordination. It is the baseline for GMT to EST calculators, charts, current GMT time, and event planning.

What is EST?

EST is Eastern Standard Time at UTC-5. When daylight saving time applies in New York, the zone becomes EDT at UTC-4.

GMT to EST Converter FAQs

What is GMT?

GMT, or Greenwich Mean Time, is the time standard historically tied to the Greenwich meridian. For practical online conversion, GMT is treated as UTC+0.

What is EST?

EST means Eastern Standard Time, UTC-5. In summer, many Eastern Time locations use EDT, which is UTC-4.

Is GMT always 5 hours ahead of EST?

GMT is 5 hours ahead of EST, but only 4 hours ahead of EDT during daylight saving time.

Does this converter handle daylight saving time?

Yes. The calculator uses the America/New_York time zone, so it shows EST or EDT based on the selected date.

What is the best time to call from GMT to EST?

For standard office hours, 14:00 to 17:00 GMT often overlaps with 9:00 to 12:00 Eastern Time, changing by one hour during DST.
