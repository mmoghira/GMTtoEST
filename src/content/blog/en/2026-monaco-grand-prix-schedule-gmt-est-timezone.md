## CEST to GMT Conversion for Monaco

Monaco uses **CEST (UTC+2)** in June.

- Convert CEST to GMT: **subtract 2 hours**
- Race example: **15:00 CEST = 13:00 GMT**

Then convert from GMT:

- GMT to BST: +1 hour
- GMT to EDT: -4 hours
- GMT to PKT: +5 hours
- GMT to IST: +5:30 hours
- GMT to AEST: +10 hours

Important for US searches: June uses **EDT**, not EST. Many users search "Monaco GP race time EST," but the correct local US East label on race day is EDT.
