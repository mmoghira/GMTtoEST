## CEST to GMT Conversion Rule for Tour de France 2026

Tour de France stage times are local to France and Spain. In July, both countries use **Central European Summer Time (CEST), UTC+2**.

**CEST to GMT: subtract 2 hours**

That means:

- **11:20 CEST = 09:20 GMT**
- **12:10 CEST = 10:10 GMT**
- **13:10 CEST = 11:10 GMT**
- **17:05 CEST = 15:05 GMT**

For other common viewer zones:

| From CEST | Conversion | Example |
|---|---:|---:|
| GMT | subtract 2 hours | 13:10 CEST = 11:10 GMT |
| BST, United Kingdom | subtract 1 hour | 13:10 CEST = 12:10 BST |
| EDT, US East | subtract 6 hours | 13:10 CEST = 07:10 EDT |
| CDT, US Central | subtract 7 hours | 13:10 CEST = 06:10 CDT |
| PDT, US West Coast | subtract 9 hours | 13:10 CEST = 04:10 PDT |
| PKT, Pakistan | add 3 hours | 13:10 CEST = 16:10 PKT |
| IST, India | add 3 hours 30 minutes | 13:10 CEST = 16:40 IST |
| AEST, eastern Australia | add 8 hours | 13:10 CEST = 21:10 AEST |

## EST or EDT: Which Eastern Time Should You Use?

If you searched for **Tour de France 2026 EST time**, use this important correction: the 2026 Tour happens in July, when New York, Washington, Toronto and most US Eastern Time locations observe **Eastern Daylight Time (EDT), UTC-4**.

So the correct conversion is:

**CEST to EDT: subtract 6 hours**

Do not subtract 7 hours unless you specifically need winter **EST**. For example, Stage 1 starts at **17:05 CEST**, which is **11:05 EDT**. It is not 10:05 EST for East Coast viewers in July.

### Stage 1: Barcelona Team Time Trial — July 4, 15:05 GMT

The race opens with a team time trial in Barcelona, returning the format to the Tour opener for the first time since 1971. The 17:05 CEST local start is especially friendly for Europe, Pakistan and India, and unusually manageable for US East Coast viewers at 11:05 EDT.

### Stage 3: First Pyrenees Test — July 6, 10:10 GMT

Stage 3 sends the race from Granollers to Les Angles and takes the peloton into the Pyrenees early. It is not the biggest mountain stage of the race, but it is the first real test of climbing form and team control.

### Stage 6: Pau to Gavarnie-Gèdre — July 9, 10:25 GMT

The first summit finish comes at Gavarnie-Gèdre. Because it arrives in week one, it could force general classification contenders to reveal form far earlier than usual.

### Stage 15: Plateau de Solaison — July 19, 11:10 GMT

Plateau de Solaison makes its Tour debut after a hard Jura and Vosges block. This is the kind of stage where fatigue, altitude and unfamiliar pacing can produce bigger gaps than expected.

### Stage 20: Le Bourg-d'Oisans to Alpe d'Huez — July 25, 09:20 GMT

Stage 20 is the monster: Alpe d'Huez on the penultimate day, after the Croix de Fer, Télégraphe, Galibier and the Col de Sarenne approach. The early start means painful alarms for the Americas, but this is the stage most likely to decide the yellow jersey.

### Stage 21: Thoiry to Paris Champs-Élysées — July 26, 14:15 GMT

The Paris finale returns to a late-afternoon European window. It starts at 16:15 CEST, converting to 14:15 GMT, 15:15 BST and 10:15 EDT.

## Tour de France 2026 Route Snapshot

The official 2026 route covers **21 stages** and **3,333 km**, with:

- **7 flat stages**
- **4 hilly stages**
- **8 mountain stages**
- **5 summit finishes**
- **1 team time trial**

The race starts with the **Grand Départ in Barcelona**, crosses the Pyrenees early, builds through the Massif Central, Vosges and Jura, and then ends with a huge Alpine block before Paris.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What time does the Tour de France 2026 start in GMT?

The opening team time trial starts at **15:05 GMT** on Saturday, July 4, 2026. Most road stages begin between **09:20 and 12:05 GMT**, while the Paris finale begins at **14:15 GMT**.

### What time is the Tour de France 2026 in EST?

Use **EDT**, not winter EST. Stage starts range from **05:20 EDT** for Stage 20 to **11:05 EDT** for the Stage 1 team time trial.

### Is the Tour de France schedule in CET or CEST?

Use **CEST, UTC+2** for July. Some cycling listings shorten the label to CET, but France and Spain are on summer time during the race.

### How do I convert CEST to GMT?

Subtract **2 hours**. A 13:10 CEST stage start is 11:10 GMT.

### What time is Stage 1 in GMT and EDT?

Stage 1 starts at **17:05 CEST**, which is **15:05 GMT**, **16:05 BST** and **11:05 EDT**.

### What time is Alpe d'Huez Stage 20 in GMT?

Stage 20 starts at **11:20 CEST**, which is **09:20 GMT**, **10:20 BST**, **05:20 EDT**, **14:20 PKT** and **14:50 IST**.

### When are the Tour de France 2026 rest days?

The rest days are **Monday, July 13** in Cantal and **Monday, July 20** in Haute-Savoie.

### Where can I watch the Tour de France 2026?
