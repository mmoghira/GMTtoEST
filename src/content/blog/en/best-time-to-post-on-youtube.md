There is no single **best time to post on YouTube** in 2027: vertical **YouTube Shorts** and dedicated **long-form videos** peak on different days under contrasting algorithms. This guide gives every study's answer, the Shorts-versus-long-form split, the day-by-day picture, and converts every slot into your audience's local time zone.

## Best Time to Post on YouTube by Day of the Week

Audience viewing routines shift across the week:

### Monday
No study gives a Monday hour; Buffer lists Monday second-best for long-form (no hour). Test SocialPilot's **2:00 PM to 4:00 PM EST** for **YouTube long-form videos**, and **12:00 PM to 2:00 PM local time** for **YouTube Shorts**.

### Tuesday
Buffer lists Tuesday second-best for long-form behind Sunday (no hour). Test SocialPilot's **2:00 PM to 4:00 PM EST** for **YouTube long-form videos**, and **1:00 PM to 3:00 PM local time** for **YouTube Shorts**.

### Wednesday
No study gives a Wednesday slot—state this plainly without inventing times. Test **2:00 PM to 4:00 PM EST** (SocialPilot) for **YouTube long-form videos**, and **12:00 PM to 3:00 PM local time** for **YouTube Shorts**.

### Thursday
No study gives a Thursday slot—state this plainly without inventing hours. Test **2:00 PM to 4:00 PM EST** (SocialPilot) for **YouTube long-form videos**, and **2:00 PM to 4:00 PM local time** for **YouTube Shorts**.

### Friday
SocialPilot highlights **2:00 PM to 4:00 PM EST** for long-form. Friday is peak for **best time to post on YouTube Shorts**: Buffer identifies **Friday at 4:00 PM local time** (within a **4:00 PM to 7:00 PM local time** window).

### Saturday
For long-form, Vista Social reports **Saturday 9:00 PM to 12:00 AM brand local time (+16% impressions)**; SocialPilot recommends **9:00 AM to 11:00 AM EST**. For **YouTube Shorts**, Buffer highlights **4:00 PM to 7:00 PM local time**.

### Sunday
Sunday is the consensus **best day to post on YouTube**. For **best time to post YouTube long-form videos**, Buffer finds **Sunday at 10:00 AM local time** best; SocialPilot names Sunday best overall (**9:00 AM to 11:00 AM EST**), and Vista records **+10% impressions**. For **YouTube Shorts**, test **10:00 AM to 1:00 PM local time**.

Country tiers are an **advertising geo-classification based on purchasing power, disposable income, and GDP per capita, used to price traffic (CPM) and pick geos for ads**. It is **NOT** a posting-time classification; no study uses tiers for YouTube posting times. Adheart reports:

* **Tier 2 (Brazil, Mexico, India, Poland, Turkey):** CPMs **$3.00–$10.00**.
* **Tier 3 (Developing nations):** CPMs **$0.10–$1.00**.

### Honest Per-Tier Publishing Guidance
* **Tier 1:** Rely on real data (Buffer Shorts/long-form split, US survey, global tables).
* **Tier 2:** Use global local-time windows (Sunday 10:00 AM long-form, Friday 4:00 PM Shorts) mapped via GMT/CET tables.
* **Tier 3:** Apply the same windows in local time, evening-weighted (**6:00 PM to 9:00 PM local time**), with an explicit limited-data caveat. Never present Tier 2 or 3 times as measured data.

## The Time-Zone Strategy: Converting YouTube Benchmarks

Publishing at "local time" is incomplete: 10:00 AM GMT London is **5:00 AM EST** New York, **2:00 AM PST** Los Angeles, and **9:00 PM AEDT** Sydney. Always anchor uploads to your primary audience hub.

### Master YouTube Time Zone Conversion Table
Headline slots during winter standard time (1 Nov 2026–14 Mar 2027; **EST = GMT−5, PST = GMT−8, CET = GMT+1, IST = GMT+5:30, AEDT = GMT+11**):

|Benchmark Slot|GMT (London)|EST (New York)|PST (Los Angeles)|CET (Paris/Berlin)|IST (Mumbai)|AEDT (Sydney)|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|**Buffer Long-Form (Sun 10am GMT)**|**10:00 AM**|5:00 AM|2:00 AM|11:00 AM|3:30 PM|9:00 PM|
|**Buffer Shorts (Fri 4pm GMT)**|**4:00 PM**|11:00 AM|8:00 AM|5:00 PM|9:30 PM|3:00 AM *(+1)*|
|**SocialPilot Weekday (2pm EST)**|7:00 PM|**2:00 PM**|11:00 AM|8:00 PM|12:30 AM *(+1)*|6:00 AM *(+1)*|
|**Vista Social Sat Night (9pm EST)**|2:00 AM *(+1)*|**9:00 PM**|6:00 PM|3:00 AM *(+1)*|7:30 AM *(+1)*|1:00 PM *(+1)*|

### Worked Example: Converting Across Continents
* **Buffer Long-Form:** Sunday 10:00 AM GMT London = **5:00 AM EST** New York = **3:30 PM IST** Mumbai.
* **Buffer Shorts:** Friday 4:00 PM GMT London = **11:00 AM EST** New York = **9:30 PM IST** Mumbai.

Convert hours using our free [GMT to EST Converter](/). See [what is EST](/blog/what-is-est/), [GMT vs EST](/blog/gmt-vs-est/), and the [meeting planner](/meeting-planner/).

### The Multi-Region Rule: Why One Post Cannot Cover the Globe
No single upload reaches North America, Europe, and Australia simultaneously: **2:00 PM EST** is **7:00 PM GMT** London, but **6:00 AM AEDT next day** Sydney.
**The Multi-Region Rule:** To reach all three regions, **post twice, about 8 hours apart** (e.g. 10:00 AM EST / 3:00 PM GMT for transatlantic viewers, and 6:00 PM EST / 10:00 AM AEDT next day for Australasia).

### The Daylight Saving Time Trap (2026 / 2027 Dates)
Audience habits do not shift; the **arithmetic offset between time zones shifts**:
* **US & Canada DST:** Ended **Sunday 1 Nov 2026**; resumes **Sunday 14 Mar 2027** (EST GMT−5 to EDT GMT−4).
* **UK & EU DST:** Ended **Sunday 25 Oct 2026**; resumes **Sunday 28 Mar 2027** (GMT to BST GMT+1).
* **Australia (NSW/VIC/SA/TAS/ACT) DST:** Started **4 Oct 2026**; ends **4 Apr 2027** (AEDT GMT+11).

During the gap week (25 Oct–1 Nov 2026), London–New York was 4 hours. A fixed schedule breaks by one hour across transitions: 2:00 PM GMT is 9:00 AM EST in winter but 10:00 AM EDT in summer. Read [what is daylight saving time](/blog/what-is-daylight-saving-time/).

## How to Find Your Best Time to Post YouTube Long-Form Videos and Shorts

Your channel's native analytics should guide your publishing calendar:

### Step 1: Analyze Native YouTube Studio Analytics
In YouTube Studio, open **Analytics > Audience** and check the purple heatmap **"When your viewers are on YouTube"**.
**Rule:** Publish **1 to 2 hours before peak purple hours** to allow video encoding (1080p, 4K), automated captioning, and content checks before traffic surges.

### Step 2: Execute a 4-Week Empirical Split Test Plan
Run a 4-week split test comparing three key slots:
* **Slot A (Long-Form):** Sunday at **10:00 AM audience local time** (Buffer).
* **Slot B (Shorts):** Friday at **4:00 PM audience local time** (Buffer).
* **Slot C (Impressions):** Saturday at **9:00 PM audience local time** (Vista Social).

Keep content consistent. Compare **watch time and click-through rate (CTR), not views alone**.
