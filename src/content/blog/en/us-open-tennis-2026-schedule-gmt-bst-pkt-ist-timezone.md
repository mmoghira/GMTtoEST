## EDT vs EST: The One-Hour Trap

The US Open is often searched as "US Open EST time", but that wording is technically wrong for the 2026 tournament dates.

New York uses:

- **EDT, Eastern Daylight Time, UTC-4**, during August and September.
- **EST, Eastern Standard Time, UTC-5**, during winter.

For this tournament, use the EDT conversion:

| Conversion | Rule during US Open 2026 |
|---|---:|
| EDT to GMT | Add 4 hours |
| EDT to BST | Add 5 hours |
| EDT to PKT | Add 9 hours |
| EDT to IST | Add 9 hours 30 minutes |
| EDT to AEST | Add 14 hours |

Example: **7:00 PM EDT is 11:00 PM GMT**, not midnight GMT. The UK is different because London is on BST in September, so the same session is midnight BST.

**Australia and East Asia:** The day session is overnight in Australia and late night in East Asia. The night session is morning in Sydney, Tokyo, Seoul and Beijing, which can be easier for weekend viewing than for weekday workdays.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What time does the US Open 2026 start in GMT?

Most day sessions begin at **11:00 AM EDT**, which is **3:00 PM GMT** and **4:00 PM BST**. Featured show-court play can start later, often around noon EDT in early rounds, so check the official order of play once it is released.

### What time is the US Open night session in GMT?

A standard US Open night session starts at **7:00 PM EDT**, which is **11:00 PM GMT**, **midnight BST**, **4:00 AM PKT** and **4:30 AM IST** the next calendar day.

### What time is the US Open men's final 2026 in GMT?

The men's singles final is scheduled for **Sunday, September 13 at 2:00 PM EDT**. That is **6:00 PM GMT**, **7:00 PM BST**, **11:00 PM PKT** and **11:30 PM IST**.

### What time is the US Open women's final 2026 in GMT?

The women's singles final is scheduled for **Saturday, September 12 at 4:00 PM EDT**. That is **8:00 PM GMT**, **9:00 PM BST**, **1:00 AM PKT** and **1:30 AM IST** on Sunday, September 13.

### Is the US Open on EST or EDT?

The US Open is on **EDT**, because New York observes daylight saving time in August and September. EDT is UTC-4. EST is UTC-5 and applies in winter.

### What time is the US Open in Pakistan?

### What time is the US Open in the UK?

### When does the 2026 US Open start and finish?

The main draw starts **Sunday, August 30, 2026** and finishes with the men's singles final on **Sunday, September 13, 2026**. Fan Week and qualifying activity begins the week before.
