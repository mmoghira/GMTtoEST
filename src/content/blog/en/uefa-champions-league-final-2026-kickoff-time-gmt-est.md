## GMT vs CEST vs EDT: Understanding the Time Differences

The UCL Final introduces three different time zones that fans frequently confuse. Here is a clear breakdown:

### CEST (Central European Summer Time): UTC+2

This is the **local Budapest time** where the match is being played. UEFA announces all kick-off times in Central European time for European competitions. The final kicks off at **18:00 CEST**.

### GMT (Greenwich Mean Time): UTC+0

GMT is the baseline world time. During summer, the UK is on BST (GMT+1), but GMT itself does not observe daylight saving. To convert from CEST to GMT: **subtract 2 hours**. So 18:00 CEST = **16:00 GMT**.

### EDT (Eastern Daylight Time): UTC-4

The US East Coast is on EDT in late May. To convert from GMT to EDT: **subtract 4 hours**. So 16:00 GMT = **12:00 PM (noon) EDT**.

The chain is simple: **CEST - 2 = GMT**, and **GMT - 4 = EDT**. The UCL Final is at noon in New York, 5 PM in London (BST), and 6 PM in Budapest.
