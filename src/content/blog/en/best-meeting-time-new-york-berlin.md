## How the Overlap is Computed

Calculating the **US Germany meeting time** uses a standard arithmetic method: intersect the two 9am–5pm business days. 9am New York is 3pm Berlin in winter (EST = GMT−5, CET = GMT+1) and 3pm Berlin in summer (EDT = GMT−4, CEST = GMT+2), so the window is the same in both seasons.

When New York offices open at 9:00am EST (14:00 UTC) in winter, Berlin is at 3:00pm CET (14:00 UTC). Berlin's business day ends at 5:00pm CET (16:00 UTC) = 11:00am EST, creating a 2-hour intersection (14:00&ndash;16:00 UTC). In summer, New York shifts to EDT (UTC-4) and Berlin to CEST (UTC+2), keeping the exact same 9:00am&ndash;11:00am EDT and 3:00pm&ndash;5:00pm CEST local hours.

| City & Time Zone | Northern Winter Offset | Northern Summer Offset | Local Business Hours | Winter UTC Window | Summer UTC Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New York (EST / EDT)** | UTC&minus;5 (EST) | UTC&minus;4 (EDT) | 9:00am&ndash;5:00pm EST / EDT | 14:00&ndash;22:00 UTC | 13:00&ndash;21:00 UTC |
| **Berlin (CET / CEST)** | UTC+1 (CET) | UTC+2 (CEST) | 9:00am&ndash;5:00pm CET / CEST | 08:00&ndash;16:00 UTC | 07:00&ndash;15:00 UTC |
| **Difference & Overlap** | **6 Hours** | **6 Hours** | &mdash; | **Shared: 14:00&ndash;16:00 UTC (2 Hours)** | **Shared: 13:00&ndash;15:00 UTC (2 Hours)** |

Because both advance clocks by 60 minutes in spring, the net difference remains 6 hours. For regional details, review [what is EST](/blog/what-is-est/), [GMT vs EST](/blog/gmt-vs-est/), and [US time zones](/blog/us-time-zones/).

## The Two Gap Weeks: 25 Oct–1 Nov 2026 and 14–28 Mar 2027

While summer and winter maintain a consistent 6-hour difference, the annual calendar contains two transitional periods: 25 October to 1 November 2026 and 14 March to 28 March 2027.

These gap weeks exist because the US changes its clocks two weeks before the EU in spring, and one week after the EU in autumn: the US springs forward on 14 March 2027 while the EU springs forward on 28 March 2027; the EU falls back on 25 October 2026 while the US falls back on 1 November 2026.

In these weeks one side has changed its clocks and the other has not, so the gap is 5 hours instead of 6. Intersecting both 9:00am to 5:00pm business days produces a 3-hour window: **9:00am–12:00pm New York (EDT) = 2:00pm–5:00pm Berlin (CET)**.

This creates a serious scheduling trap for standing commitments:

> [!WARNING]
> A recurring meeting fixed at 3:00pm Berlin time is **10:00am in New York** during these weeks instead of 9:00am. Team members expecting the usual 9:00am EDT call will join an hour early or miss the sync if unaware of the temporary 5-hour offset.

Conversely, a meeting fixed at 9:00am New York time lands at 2:00pm Berlin time during these weeks instead of 3:00pm CET. For broader multi-zone strategies, see our pillar guide on [how to schedule a meeting across time zones](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-meeting-across-time-zones/).

Because New York and Berlin share a concentrated 2-hour window, choosing the right slot within the overlap preserves employee energy and workflow:

- **Best Time &mdash; 10:00am New York = 4:00pm Berlin:** This sits in the middle of the overlap when both sides are alert during regular business hours.
- **Secondary Window &mdash; 9:00am New York = 3:00pm Berlin:** Inside the overlap, suitable for brief daily standups or tactical handoffs.
- **Worst Time &mdash; Before 9:00am New York:** An 8:00am EST / EDT meeting forces New York to join before core hours, while Berlin is already past 3:00pm CET / CEST.

## The DST Trap: March 2027 Worked Example

The most common transatlantic scheduling error is misunderstanding daylight saving time: **a wall-clock time does not move, only the offset shifts.**

When an invitation specifies 3:00pm Berlin time, Berlin's wall clock remains at 3:00pm. Because the US and Germany adjust clocks on different dates, the offset shifts between 6 hours and 5 hours.

Consider a meeting scheduled at **3:00pm Berlin time** across March 2027:

1. **Before 14 March 2027 (Winter):** Berlin is on CET (UTC+1) and New York is on EST (UTC-5) (6-hour gap). A meeting at 3:00pm Berlin is **9:00am New York**.
2. **14 March to 28 March 2027 (Gap Weeks):** The US springs forward to EDT (UTC-4) on 14 March 2027 while Germany remains on CET (UTC+1) (5-hour gap). A meeting at 3:00pm Berlin is **10:00am New York**.
3. **After 28 March 2027 (Summer):** Germany springs forward to CEST (UTC+2) on 28 March 2027 while New York remains on EDT (UTC-4) (6-hour gap). A meeting at 3:00pm Berlin returns to **9:00am New York**.

Worked examples to verify:
- **10:00am New York = 4:00pm Berlin**, in both winter (EST to CET) and summer (EDT to CEST).
- **3:00pm Berlin = 9:00am New York** in winter (CET to EST) and summer (CEST to EDT), but **10:00am New York** during the two gap weeks (CET to EDT).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best time for a meeting between New York and Berlin?
The best time is **10:00am EST (4:00pm CET)** in winter and **10:00am EDT (4:00pm CEST)** in summer. This sits squarely in the middle of the 2-hour overlap when both sides are alert during regular business hours.

### How many hours do New York and Berlin overlap?
New York and Berlin overlap for **2 hours** (9:00am–11:00am New York = 3:00pm–5:00pm Berlin) in both winter and summer. During the two annual gap weeks, the overlap expands to **3 hours** (9:00am–12:00pm New York = 2:00pm–5:00pm Berlin).

### Is the overlap the same in summer and winter?
Yes. In winter, New York is UTC-5 (EST) and Berlin is UTC+1 (CET), a 6-hour gap. In summer, New York is UTC-4 (EDT) and Berlin is UTC+2 (CEST), keeping the exact same 6-hour gap and 2-hour business overlap.

### What are the gap weeks between New York and Berlin?
The gap weeks are **25 Oct &ndash; 1 Nov 2026** and **14 &ndash; 28 Mar 2027**, when the United States and the European Union switch clocks on different Sundays. Because one side has shifted and the other has not, the gap narrows to 5 hours.

### What is the worst time to book a New York–Berlin meeting?
The worst times are before 9:00am EST / EDT in New York (Berlin is already past 3:00pm CET / CEST) and after 11:00am EST / EDT in New York (Berlin is past 5:00pm CET / CEST at the end of its working day).

### How do I schedule a recurring meeting between New York and Berlin?
Book inside 9:00am–11:00am EST / EDT (3:00pm–5:00pm CET / CEST), state both local times in the invite, and adjust for the 1-hour relative shift during the March and October gap weeks.

### Does daylight saving time change our meeting time?
A wall-clock time does not move, but the UTC offset shifts. In the two gap weeks, the difference narrows to 5 hours, shifting recurring meetings by one hour relative to the other city's clock.

### What time is 10am in New York in Berlin?
