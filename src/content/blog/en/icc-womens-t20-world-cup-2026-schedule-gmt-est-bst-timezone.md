## BST to GMT: The Critical Conversion

All official UK match times are listed in **BST (British Summer Time)**. During June and July, BST is **UTC+1**, while GMT is **UTC+0**.

**BST to GMT rule: subtract 1 hour.**

That means:

- **10:30 BST = 09:30 GMT**
- **14:30 BST = 13:30 GMT**
- **18:30 BST = 17:30 GMT**

This is the most common mistake international viewers make. If a fixture says 18:30 in England, do not read that as 18:30 GMT. The correct GMT time is 17:30.

## Viewing Tips for International Fans

Convert from the published BST time first. If your broadcaster lists a local time, compare it against the ICC fixture time and the date to avoid DST mistakes.

Watch for next-day conversions. Evening matches at 18:30 BST are still the same day in GMT, Europe, Africa, the Americas, Pakistan and India, but they become **03:30 the next morning in AEST**.

Use EDT, not EST, for North America. During this tournament, a 14:30 BST match is 09:30 EDT. If you convert using winter EST, you will be one hour late.

Bookmark the main conversion slots. Once you know 10:30, 14:30 and 18:30 BST, most of the group stage becomes easy to plan.
