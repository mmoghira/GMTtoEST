## BST vs GMT — The Wimbledon Timezone Trap

This is crucial and often misunderstood. Wimbledon publishes all match times in **BST (British Summer Time)**, which is **UTC+1** during summer. But GMT is **UTC+0** — meaning BST is one hour ahead of GMT.

So when Wimbledon announces Centre Court starts at **1:30 PM BST**, the correct GMT time is **12:30 PM GMT** — not 1:30 PM.

**The key conversion rule for Wimbledon:**

- **BST to GMT:** subtract 1 hour
- **BST to EDT (US East):** subtract 5 hours
- **BST to PKT (Pakistan):** add 4 hours
- **BST to IST (India):** add 4.5 hours
- **BST to AEST (Australia East):** add 9 hours

## Wimbledon's Unique Traditions That Affect the Schedule

Unlike any other Grand Slam, Wimbledon has several traditions that international fans should know before planning their viewing:

**The defending champion opens Centre Court.** By tradition, the defending Gentlemen's Singles champion opens play on Centre Court at 1:30 PM on Monday, followed by the defending Ladies' Singles champion on Tuesday. This means Jannik Sinner will be the first player on Centre Court on June 29 — a high-profile opening match for international viewers.

**Middle Sunday is now a full match day.** The traditional "Middle Sunday" rest day was permanently retired in 2022, transforming the middle weekend into one of the most action-packed periods in sports. There is no longer a day off during the fortnight.

**The Order of Play is released late the night before.** The AELTC confirms the final order of play only on the evening before, and the schedule remains subject to change at the Referee's discretion. This means you will know which player is on which court only the night before each session — not days in advance.

**Rain delays are common.** Wimbledon is played on outdoor grass courts in the unpredictable English summer. The retractable roof on Centre Court and No. 1 Court means play continues through rain on those two courts, but all outside courts stop when it rains. Schedule accordingly — matches can run well into the evening on disrupted days.

## Qualifying Week — Before the Main Draw

Before the main tournament begins at the All England Club, qualifying week takes place at the Community Sport Centre in Roehampton. It is expected to begin around **Monday, June 22, 2026**, approximately one week before the main draw.

Qualifying is not televised widely but is a fascinating window into the tournament for hardcore fans. Players fight for the final spots in the 128-player singles draws, often producing intense matches that receive no Centre Court glamour but enormous competitive quality.
