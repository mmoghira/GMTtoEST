LA28 lists schedule times in Pacific Time. In July, Pacific Time is **PDT (UTC-7)**.

**PDT to GMT rule: add 7 hours.**

From there, convert to your local zone:

## Best Timezones for Watching LA 2028 Live

### Pakistan and India

South Asia gets one of the best LA 2028 viewing patterns for morning Olympic sessions. A **9:00 AM PT** start becomes **9:00 PM PKT** and **9:30 PM IST**, which is ideal for live evening viewing.

The harder part is LA afternoon competition. A **2:00 PM PT** session becomes **2:00 AM PKT** and **2:30 AM IST** the next day. Late US finals can be surprisingly watchable, though: a **7:00 PM PT** final is **7:00 AM PKT** and **7:30 AM IST**.

### United Kingdom and Ireland

UK viewers should plan around LA morning and early afternoon sessions. A **9:00 AM PT** event is **5:00 PM BST**, and a **2:00 PM PT** event is **10:00 PM BST**.

The difficult window is the American evening session. A **7:00 PM PT** final is **3:00 AM BST** the next day, and an **8:00 PM PT** final is **4:00 AM BST**. For the UK, LA 2028 will be a mix of live early-evening sessions and next-morning highlights.

### Europe and Africa

Central Europe is one hour later than the UK, so morning sessions in Los Angeles often become a comfortable **6:00 PM CEST**. West Africa is close to the UK pattern, with **9:00 AM PT** converting to **5:00 PM WAT**.

East Africa gets later but still usable evening times for LA morning sessions: **9:00 AM PT** is **7:00 PM EAT**.

### Australia and East Asia

Australia has a split experience. LA morning sessions are difficult because **9:00 AM PT** is **2:00 AM AEST** the next day. LA afternoon sessions are easier, with **2:00 PM PT** converting to **7:00 AM AEST**. LA evening finals are very friendly for Australia, often landing around late morning or early afternoon.

Japan, Korea and eastern China see LA morning events after midnight, but evening LA finals become late morning on the next calendar day.

### United States and Canada

North America gets the simplest schedule. New York and the US East Coast are three hours ahead of Los Angeles, so **5:00 PM PT** is **8:00 PM EDT** and **7:00 PM PT** is **10:00 PM EDT**. Evening finals are late on the East Coast but still realistic for major Olympic nights.
