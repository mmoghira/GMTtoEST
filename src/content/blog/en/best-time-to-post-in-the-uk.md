This is a region guide, not a platform guide. The **best time to post in the UK** depends on measured British audience activity from Sprout Social's UK study (230 million UK engagements, times in GMT).

Thursday evening represents the UK's best window, while Saturday is consistently the worst day. Because updates from London reach audiences worldwide, every UK slot in this guide is converted for US and Australian audiences. Planning your **best time to post UK 2027** schedule and determining the **best time to post on social media UK** followers respond to requires coordinating British peak hours with global time zones.

## Best Times by Audience Location: Converting UK Evening Slots

UK evening slots distribute across overseas markets as follows:

*   **UK Readers:** GMT slots require no conversion: use the general window (**Thursday 4pm to 11pm GMT**) or weekday evenings (**5pm to 9pm GMT**).
*   **US Readers:** UK evenings coincide with US workday hours: Thursday 4pm GMT is **11am EST / 8am PST**, and Thursday 11pm GMT is **6pm EST / 3pm PST**.
*   **Australian Readers:** UK evenings land the next morning in Australia: Thursday 4pm GMT is **3am AEDT next day**, and Thursday 11pm GMT is **10am AEDT next day**.
*   **Canada:** No separate Canadian data exists; target Canadian provinces using US Eastern/Pacific times (**11am to 6pm EST / 8am to 3pm PST**).
*   **Western Europe (CET):** The UK is 1 hour behind Central European Time (add 1 hour): Thursday 4–11pm GMT corresponds to **5pm to 12am CET**.
*   **Asia & Latin America:** No UK-specific data exists — say so plainly. Use IST (**9:30pm to 4:30am IST next day**) for India, and local evening slots for Latin America.

### Worked Multi-Zone Example
The UK general window **Thursday 4pm to 11pm GMT** equals **11am to 6pm EST** in New York, **8am to 3pm PST** in California, and **9:30pm to 4:30am IST next day** in Mumbai. A single UK evening post engages American audiences throughout their workday.

### The Multi-Region Rule: Post Twice, 8 Hours Apart
A single post cannot reach the US, UK, and Australia simultaneously: 8pm GMT in London is 3pm EST in New York and 7am AEDT next day in Sydney.

**Multi-Region Rule:** Post twice, roughly 8 hours apart:
1.  **Slot 1 (4pm GMT / 11am EST):** Reaches the UK evening and US morning.
2.  **Slot 2 (12am GMT / 7pm EST / 11am AEDT next day):** Captures the US evening and Australian morning. Plan schedules with our [Meeting Planner](/meeting-planner/).

### The Daylight Saving Time (DST) Trap
Audience habits remain fixed to clock time; what changes is the mathematical offset between zones:
*   **US & Canada:** DST ended Sun 1 Nov 2026; resumes Sun 14 Mar 2027.
*   **UK & Europe:** BST/CEST ended Sun 25 Oct 2026; resumes Sun 28 Mar 2027.
*   **Australia (NSW/VIC/SA/TAS/ACT):** DST began 4 Oct 2026; ends 4 Apr 2027.

The UK switches clocks a week earlier in autumn (25 Oct vs 1 Nov 2026) and two weeks later in spring (28 Mar vs 14 Mar 2027). During these gap weeks, the NY–London difference is 4 hours (4pm GMT is 12pm EDT). Standard winter offsets: EST = GMT−5, EDT = GMT−4, PST = GMT−8, CET = GMT+1, IST = GMT+5:30, AEDT = GMT+11.
