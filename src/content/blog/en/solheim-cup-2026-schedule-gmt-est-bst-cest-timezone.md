## CEST to GMT: The Key Conversion

The Netherlands uses **CEST (Central European Summer Time)** in September. CEST is **UTC+2**, while GMT is **UTC+0**.

**Solheim Cup conversion rule: CEST - 2 hours = GMT.**

That means:

- **8:00 AM CEST = 6:00 AM GMT**
- **8:30 AM CEST = 6:30 AM GMT**
- **1:00 PM CEST = 11:00 AM GMT**
- **1:30 PM CEST = 11:30 AM GMT**

For UK viewers, London is on **BST (UTC+1)** in September, so CEST is only one hour ahead of the UK. An 8:00 AM CEST start would be 7:00 AM BST.

For US viewers, searches often say "EST," but New York and the US East Coast are on **EDT (UTC-4)** in September. An 8:00 AM CEST start would be 2:00 AM EDT, not 3:00 AM EST.

## Expected Session Format: Foursomes, Fourballs and Singles

The Solheim Cup uses a match-play team format similar to the Ryder Cup. Each match is worth one point, and a halved match gives each side half a point.

| Format | When used in 2026 | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Foursomes | Friday morning and Saturday morning | Two-player teams use one ball and alternate shots |
| Fourballs | Friday afternoon and Saturday afternoon | Two-player teams each play their own ball; the better score counts |
| Singles | Sunday | One Europe player faces one USA player head-to-head |

Across the three match days, 28 points are available. A team needs 14.5 points to win outright. If the final score is tied, the defending champion retains the Cup.
