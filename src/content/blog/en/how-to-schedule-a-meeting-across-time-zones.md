This guide provides one reliable 4-step method that works for any two cities on Earth, explains how to handle daylight saving gap weeks, and details the verified **time zone overlap** windows for the five most common international business pairs.

## The 4-Step Method to Schedule a Meeting Across Time Zones

Finding the **best time to schedule a meeting across time zones** follows four mathematical steps:

### Step 1: Find Both Offsets (UTC Baseline)

Every standard time zone is defined by its offset from Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). Determine each city's exact UTC offset during winter (standard time) and summer (daylight saving time):

| City | Winter Zone & Offset | Summer Zone & Offset | Observes Daylight Saving? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New York** | EST (UTC&minus;5) | EDT (UTC&minus;4) | Yes (Mar to Nov) |
| **London** | GMT (UTC+0) | BST (UTC+1) | Yes (Mar to Oct) |
| **Berlin** | CET (UTC+1) | CEST (UTC+2) | Yes (Mar to Oct) |
| **Mumbai** | IST (UTC+5:30) | IST (UTC+5:30) | **No** (Year-round UTC+5:30) |
| **Sydney** | AEST (UTC+10) | AEDT (UTC+11) | Yes (Oct to Apr) |

Reviewing regional boundaries—such as [What Is EST?](/blog/what-is-est/), [GMT vs EST](/blog/gmt-vs-est/), [US Time Zones](/blog/us-time-zones/), and [Canada Time Zones](/blog/canada-time-zones/)—establishes the arithmetic foundation for calculating offsets.

### Step 2: Intersect the Two 9:00am–5:00pm Business Days

The true **time zone overlap** is the part of the day that is 9:00am to 5:00pm local hours on both sides simultaneously:
1. Express 9:00am–5:00pm working hours in UTC.
2. In winter, New York (EST, UTC&minus;5) works 14:00–22:00 UTC; London (GMT, UTC+0) works 09:00–17:00 UTC.
3. Intersect spans: 14:00–17:00 UTC falls inside both workdays.
4. Convert back: 9:00am–12:00pm EST in New York equals 2:00pm–5:00pm GMT in London (a 3-hour overlap).
5. If UTC spans do not intersect, zero 9–5 overlap exists.

### Step 3: Check the Daylight Saving Gap Weeks

A recurring meeting fixed in one zone shifts by one hour when clocks change. A wall-clock time does not move; what moves is the offset between zones: the gap between two cities shifts by one hour at each clock change.

Key transition dates:
- **US and Canada**: DST ended Sun 1 Nov 2026; starts Sun 14 Mar 2027.
- **UK and EU**: Summer time ended Sun 25 Oct 2026; starts Sun 28 Mar 2027.
- **Australia (NSW/VIC/SA/TAS/ACT)**: DST started Sun 4 Oct 2026; ends Sun 4 Apr 2027.

#### Worked Example: The Recurring Meeting Gap Shift
A weekly sync fixed at 2:00pm London time:
- **Winter**: 2:00pm GMT = 9:00am EST in New York (5h gap).
- **Summer**: 2:00pm BST = 9:00am EDT in New York (5h gap).
- **Autumn Gap Week (25 Oct &ndash; 1 Nov 2026)**: London switches to GMT (UTC+0) while New York stays on EDT (UTC&minus;4). The gap is 4h: 2:00pm GMT is **10:00am EDT** in New York.
- **Spring Gap Weeks (14&ndash;28 Mar 2027)**: New York jumps to EDT (UTC&minus;4) while London stays on GMT (UTC+0). The gap is 4h: 2:00pm GMT is **10:00am EDT** in New York.

See [What Is Daylight Saving Time?](/blog/what-is-daylight-saving-time/) for seasonal offset details.

### Step 4: Pick the Window Inside the Overlap

Keep calls inside the overlap:
- **Prefer Late Morning and Afternoon**: Late morning on one side and afternoon on the other (e.g., 10:00am EST in New York = 3:00pm GMT in London).
- **Avoid Edges**: Keep meetings inside the overlap (9:00am EST catches staff arriving; 4:30pm GMT cuts into evening).
- **Buffer by 30 Minutes**: Cushion against overruns into personal evening time.

## When There Is No Overlap: Least-Bad Windows, Async-First, and Core Hours

When cities sit 8 to 16 time zones apart, no 9–5 overlap exists. Admitting this allows teams to set realistic agreements instead of forcing impossible live calls.

Manage zero-overlap pairings using three practical strategies:

### 1. Identify Least-Bad Windows
A least-bad window asks one side to stretch slightly while keeping the other within daytime hours. For New York and Mumbai, New York starting at 7:30am EST allows Mumbai colleagues to wrap up by 6:00pm IST, avoiding late-night calls.

### 2. The Async-First Rule
Not every discussion requires a live **meeting across time zones**. Teams spanning extreme offsets should adopt an async-first workflow: recorded video (5-minute Loom screen recordings), shared docs (proposals with threaded comments for review during local daylight), and decisions in writing (summarized in team channels). Reserve live meetings strictly for strategic alignment or complex troubleshooting.

### 3. Establish "Core Hours"
Teams define **core hours**—an agreed 1–2 hour daily window created by shifting one side's working day:
- Mumbai working 5:30pm–7:30pm IST overlaps New York 7:00am–9:00am EST in winter.
- London working 10:00am–6:30pm GMT creates an expanded afternoon overlap with the US West Coast.

Core hours are mutually agreed so staff disconnect guilt-free outside the window.

## Rotating-Schedule Fairness: Distribute the Burden

Do not make the same side take the evening. If US teams always pick 9:00am EST, Mumbai colleagues join at 7:30pm IST every week, causing burnout.

Rotate the meeting time weekly or monthly using a fair schedule:

### Sample 4-Week Rotation Matrix (New York &ndash; Mumbai)

| Week | Slot (New York Local) | Slot (Mumbai Local) | Regional Burden |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1** | Tuesday 8:00am EST | Tuesday 6:30pm IST | New York morning / Mumbai evening |
| **Week 2** | Thursday 8:30pm EST | Friday 7:00am IST | Mumbai morning / New York evening |
| **Week 3** | Tuesday 8:00am EST | Tuesday 6:30pm IST | New York morning / Mumbai evening |
| **Week 4** | Thursday 8:30pm EST | Friday 7:00am IST | Mumbai morning / New York evening |

*Practical Rota Note*: A standard Mumbai morning slot (e.g., 9:30am IST) requires New York attendees to join at 11:00pm EST the previous night—a very early or late start. Because of this, most teams rotate between two evening slots instead of scheduling midnight calls.

Say who takes the bad slot and how often, in writing, before the rota starts. Cap evening calls at 30–45 minutes, and let late attendees start later the next day.

## Global Meeting Etiquette and Communication Standards

Mastering time zone math must be paired with considerate communication:
1. **Never Default to US Hours**: Avoid assuming counterparts operate on US time; ask what times suit their local rhythm.
2. **State Time Zones in Every Invite**: Include both sides' local times and three-letter abbreviations (e.g., 10:00am EST / 3:00pm GMT). Never publish a bare time without a zone.
3. **State the Calendar Date Explicitly**: Because wide offsets span the date line, New York evening is Sydney the next morning (e.g., Tuesday 6:00pm EST = Wednesday 10:00am AEDT). Always list the date for both locations.
4. **Send Invites Showing Both Local Times**: Display both attendees' local wall-clock times in the meeting body so all participants can confirm schedules at a glance.
5. **Confirm the Day Before After a Clock Change**: Send a reminder 24 hours prior during DST gap weeks to confirm alignment on shifted local times.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if there is no overlap between our time zones?
Schedule during least-bad windows (e.g. 7:30am EST = 6:00pm IST), agree on 1–2 hour core hours, or switch to async video and documentation.

### How do I schedule a recurring meeting across time zones?
Anchor to one city's wall clock, calculate partner times, check DST gap weeks, and confirm invites before clock-change dates.

### Does daylight saving time change my recurring meeting?
DST changes the UTC offset between regions, not work habits. Meetings shift by one hour during asynchronous gap weeks.

### Which time zone should the invite state?
State both local times with zone abbreviations (such as EST and GMT) and the calendar date, as wide gaps cross calendar days.

### How often should we rotate the meeting time?
Rotate weekly or monthly so evening calls are shared equitably across distributed hubs.

### What are core hours?
Core hours are an agreed daily 1–2 hour window where distributed teams adjust hours to create a shared live collaboration band.

### Is it better to meet in the morning or the evening?
Meet late morning for the earlier zone and afternoon for the later zone, avoiding starts before 8:00am or evenings after 8:00pm.

### How do I schedule a meeting between the US and Asia?
Schedule during the least-bad window: 7:00am–11:00am EST in New York, which aligns with 5:30pm–9:30pm IST in Mumbai.

### How do I schedule a meeting between the US and Europe?
Use their 2- to 3-hour overlap: New York 9:00am–12:00pm EST = London 2:00pm–5:00pm GMT, or New York 9:00am–11:00am EST = Berlin 3:00pm–5:00pm CET.

### How do I find the overlap between any two cities?
Find both UTC offsets, plot 9:00am–5:00pm local hours on a 24-hour UTC timeline, calculate intersecting hours, and check active DST gap weeks.
