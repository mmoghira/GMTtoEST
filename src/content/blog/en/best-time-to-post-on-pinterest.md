There is no single universal **best time to post on Pinterest** — the major studies disagree by hours, and clash on whether late morning work breaks or evening downtime win. While Pinterest operates as a visual discovery search engine where pins accumulate saves over months, initial distribution velocity depends on posting when active pinners engage. Determining the **best time to post on Pinterest 2027** requires examining each empirical study, understanding the divide between late morning scrollers and evening planners, and converting every slot into your audience's local time zone.

## Why the Studies Disagree: Methodology and Sample Differences

Benchmark reports diverge on the **best time to post pins** because each tracks different user behaviors across distinct datasets:

|Study|Sample & Dates Analyzed|Primary Metric Tracked|Headline Benchmark Slot(s)|Time Zone Basis|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|**Sprout Social (2026)**|~2bn engagements (27 Nov 2025–27 Feb 2026)|Total engagement volume|Tue–Thu 10am–1pm local|Audience local time|
|**Hootsuite**|1M+ posts (118 countries)|Interaction volume & clicks|Tue & Fri 12pm local|Time-zone agnostic|
|**SocialPilot (2026)**|~7M posts (2025–2026)|Cross-network engagement|Evenings: 8pm, 4pm, 9pm|Account local time|

Two major analytics providers — **Buffer and Vista Social do NOT cover Pinterest** in their benchmark studies. Say this plainly: neither Buffer nor Vista tracks Pinterest data, and neither should be quoted as a Pinterest source.

In plain words: **total engagement favours late-morning hours** (10am–1pm local) when millions scroll during office breaks or gather design inspiration. Conversely, **SocialPilot's evening slots reflect a different sample and method**, tracking post-dinner curation when pinners build personal project boards. Pinterest is also the least-covered platform in the big studies, so the evidence base is thinner than for Facebook or Instagram — say that plainly.

## Best Time to Post on Pinterest by Day of the Week

Audience routines shift through the week as pinners transition from workday desk browsing to Sunday planning:

### Monday
Monday brings focused project planning. Sprout records peak volume at **10am–12pm local** and **2–3pm local**; Hootsuite pinpoints **10am local**; SocialPilot designates Monday a best day.

### Tuesday
Tuesday provides multi-study consensus for the **best day to post on Pinterest**. Sprout measures steady volume from **10am to 1pm local** (plus **7am local** check-ins); Hootsuite marks **12pm local** as a headline peak; SocialPilot ranks Tuesday among its best days.

### Wednesday
Wednesday maintains strong velocity: Sprout measures continuous activity from **8am to 1pm local** and at **3pm local**; Hootsuite records its peak at **10am local**.

### Thursday
Thursday reflects weekend prep: Sprout measures elevated volume at **10–11am local** and **2–4pm local**; Hootsuite identifies an evening peak at **6pm local**.

### Friday
Friday activity concentrates early: Sprout captures **10–11am local**; Hootsuite tracks a peak at **12pm local** before evening activity declines.

### Saturday
Saturday reveals methodology divergence: Hootsuite records **1pm local**, while Sprout rates weekends lowest for engagement volume.

### Sunday
Sunday illustrates contrasting findings: SocialPilot designates Sunday a best day and Hootsuite records **4pm local**, whereas Sprout measures weekends as lowest in total volume.

## The Time-Zone Strategy: Converting Slots Across Global Markets

Publishing at "local time" is an incomplete answer. A Pin published at **10am GMT** in London goes live at **5am EST** in New York, **2am PST** in Los Angeles, and **9pm AEDT** in Sydney. Always anchor updates to your primary target audience hub.

### Master Pinterest Time Zone Conversion Table
Headline benchmark slots during winter standard time (1 Nov 2026–14 Mar 2027; **EST = GMT−5, PST = GMT−8, CET = GMT+1, IST = GMT+5:30, AEDT = GMT+11**):

|Headline Slot|GMT (London)|EST (New York)|PST (San Francisco)|CET (Paris/Berlin)|IST (Mumbai)|AEDT (Sydney)|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|**Sprout Midday Start**|**10am GMT**|5am EST|2am PST|11am CET|3:30pm IST|9pm AEDT|
|**Sprout Midday End**|**1pm GMT**|8am EST|5am PST|2pm CET|6:30pm IST|12am AEDT *(+1)*|
|**Hootsuite Midday Peak**|**12pm GMT**|7am EST|4am PST|1pm CET|5:30pm IST|11pm AEDT|
|**SocialPilot Evening Peak**|**8pm GMT**|3pm EST|12pm PST|9pm CET|1:30am IST *(+1)*|7am AEDT *(+1)*|

*(+1) indicates following calendar day.* Verify custom hours using our [GMT to EST converter](https://gmttoestconverter.com/).

### Worked Multi-Zone Example
Sprout's primary Tuesday through Thursday window of **10am–1pm London (GMT)** demonstrates cross-border offsets:
*   **10am GMT London:** Equals **5am EST** New York, **2am PST** San Francisco, **11am CET** Paris, and **3:30pm IST** Mumbai.
*   **1pm GMT London:** Equals **8am EST** New York, **5am PST** on the US West Coast, and **6:30pm IST** Mumbai.

To reach US East Coast pinners during their peak 10am–1pm EST window, UK and European creators must post between **3pm and 6pm GMT**.

### The Multi-Region Rule: Post Twice, 8 Hours Apart
A single Pin cannot hit the US, UK, and Australia simultaneously. When New York is active at 1pm EST, London is at 6pm GMT and Sydney is at 5am AEDT the next day.

**Multi-Region Rule:** Publish fresh Pins twice daily, roughly 8 hours apart: **1pm GMT** (European afternoon / US morning) and **9pm GMT** (US afternoon / Sydney 8am AEDT next day). Plan multi-zone schedules using our [Meeting Planner](/meeting-planner/) and [GMT to EST Converter](/).

### The Daylight Saving Time (DST) Trap
Daylight Saving Time does not change when people scroll; audience habits stay fixed to local clock time. What changes is the **mathematical offset** between international zones:
*   **US & Canada:** DST ended Sun 1 Nov 2026; resumes Sun 14 Mar 2027.
*   **UK & Europe:** BST/CEST ended Sun 25 Oct 2026; resumes Sun 28 Mar 2027.
*   **Australia (NSW/VIC/SA/TAS/ACT):** DST began 4 Oct 2026, ends 4 Apr 2027.

During the gap week 25 Oct–1 Nov 2026, London was only 4 hours ahead of New York (2pm GMT was 10am EDT, not 9am EST). See [What Is EST](/blog/what-is-est/), [GMT vs EST](/blog/gmt-vs-est/), and [What Is Daylight Saving Time](/blog/what-is-daylight-saving-time/).

## How to Find Your Brand’s Best Time to Post Pins for Maximum Engagement

Macro benchmark reports provide starting hypotheses, but your account requires empirical testing. Follow this 4-week framework using native platform analytics.

### Step 1: Audit Native Pinterest Analytics
Open your Pinterest Business Hub (`analytics.pinterest.com`). Under Overview, filter by **Saves** and **Outbound Clicks** rather than impressions alone. Identify your top 20 Pins from the past 90 days. Group their original publishing times into morning (8–11am), midday (12–3pm), and evening (7–10pm) cohorts in your audience's dominant time zone.

### Step 2: The 4-Week Split Test Plan
Execute a controlled test comparing headline study benchmarks across identical content formats:
*   **Week 1 (Late Morning Baseline):** Post Tuesday at **10am audience local time** (Sprout benchmark).
*   **Week 2 (Early Morning Test):** Post Wednesday at **8am audience local time** (Sprout early slot).
*   **Week 3 (Midday Peak Check):** Post Friday at **12pm audience local time** (Hootsuite benchmark).
*   **Week 4 (Evening Curation Test):** Post Sunday at **8pm audience local time** (SocialPilot benchmark).

### Step 3: Compare Saves and Outbound Clicks
Evaluate performance using high-intent conversion metrics:
*   **Outbound Clicks:** Measures traffic delivery and purchase intent.
*   **Save Rate (Repins):** Signals algorithmic authority and long-term search ranking.
*   **Avoid Impressions Alone:** Impressions measure passive feed display, whereas saves and clicks confirm active audience engagement.
