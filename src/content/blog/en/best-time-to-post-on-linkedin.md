## Why the Studies Disagree: Methodology and Metric Breakdown

In plain words: **total engagement favours busy workday hours** when professionals sit at desks, **median engagement rate favours quiet hours** with lower feed competition, and **views/impressions favour low-competition hours**. Buffer notes a real shift: peak posting sat firmly within the workday in 2025, but afternoons and evenings (3–6pm) now win. Vista finds Sunday best on impressions (+16% at 3–6pm, +7% overall), while every engagement study calls weekends worst.

## Best Time to Post on LinkedIn by Day of the Week

Audience professional routines evolve across the workweek:

### Monday
Vista Social identifies Monday as the best weekday for impressions, but provides no hour—report without inventing times; no other study gives Monday. Professionals spend Monday mornings clearing backlogs. If posting, test **10:00 AM to 12:00 PM audience local time**, avoiding dawn.

### Tuesday
Sprout Social records sustained engagement from **11:00 AM to 5:00 PM audience local time**. SocialPilot captures mid-morning breaks (**10:00 AM to 12:00 PM local time**). Hootsuite records an executive dawn spike from **4:00 AM to 6:00 AM local time**.

### Wednesday
Wednesday is the consensus **best day to post on LinkedIn**. Sprout measures heavy engagement from **11:00 AM to 4:00 PM audience local time**. Buffer isolates **Wednesday at 4:00 PM audience local time** as the top slot for median engagement rate. Hootsuite and SocialPilot confirm midday traffic (**10:00 AM–12:00 PM local time**).

### Thursday
Sprout records active windows at **11:00 AM and 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM audience local time**. SocialPilot captures steady mid-morning engagement from **10:00 AM to 12:00 PM local time**.

### Friday
Sprout records peaks at **11:00 AM and 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM audience local time** before afternoon volume drops. Buffer records top engagement rates on **Friday at 3:00 PM and 4:00 PM audience local time** as competition slows.

### Saturday
Saturday records lowest engagement across Sprout, Hootsuite, and SocialPilot. Buffer's 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM local window covers Saturday for quiet reach, though business dialogue remains minimal.

### Sunday
Sunday splits by metric: Sprout and SocialPilot call Sunday worst for engagement. Vista Social finds Sunday delivers **+7% impressions overall** and peaks at **3:00 PM to 6:00 PM audience local time** (+16% reach). Buffer's 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM window also covers Sunday.

Sprout Social UK analyzed 230M non-network engagements in GMT. For the **best time to post on LinkedIn UK**, data identifies **Thursday from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM GMT** and **Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM GMT**, capturing daytime work and evening commutes.

### Canada
No dedicated multi-million post study tracks Canadian LinkedIn users exclusively—say so plainly. Workflows mirror US schedules. Target **Tuesday through Thursday 10am to 3pm in the recipient's provincial zone** (EST in Ontario/Quebec, CST in Manitoba, MST in Alberta, PST in British Columbia).

### Australia

When targeting multi-tier audiences:

## The Time-Zone Strategy: Converting Slots Across Global Financial Hubs

Publishing at "local time" is incomplete: 11:00 AM GMT London is **6:00 AM EST** New York, **3:00 AM PST** Los Angeles, and **10:00 PM AEDT** Sydney. Always anchor updates to your primary audience hub.

### Master LinkedIn Time Zone Conversion Table
Headline slots during winter standard time (1 Nov 2026–14 Mar 2027; **EST = GMT−5, PST = GMT−8, CET = GMT+1, IST = GMT+5:30, AEDT = GMT+11**):

|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|

|**US Afternoon Shift (3pm EST)**|8pm GMT|**3pm EST**|12pm PST|9pm CET|1:30am IST *(+1)*|7am AEDT *(+1)*|

*(+1) indicates following calendar day.* Verify custom hours with our [GMT to EST converter](https://gmttoestconverter.com/).

### Worked Multi-Zone Examples
* **Sprout Workday Window (Tue–Thu 11am–5pm in London):** Equals **6:00 AM to 12:00 PM EST** in New York, **4:30 PM to 10:30 PM IST** in Mumbai, and overnight (**10:00 PM to 4:00 AM AEDT**) in Sydney.

### The Multi-Region Rule: Why You Cannot Target All Audiences in One Post
A single post cannot hit peak hours in North America, Europe, and Australia simultaneously. When managing an international audience, **publish twice, approximately 8 hours apart**, rather than choosing a weak compromise hour.

### The Daylight Saving Time (DST) Trap
DST does not change when professionals browse LinkedIn; work routines remain pinned to local wall clocks. What changes is the **mathematical offset** between international financial hubs:
* **US & Canada:** DST ended Sun 1 Nov 2026; resumes Sun 14 Mar 2027.
* **UK & Europe:** BST/CEST ended Sun 25 Oct 2026; resumes Sun 28 Mar 2027.
* **Australia (NSW/VIC/SA/TAS/ACT):** DST started 4 Oct 2026, ends 4 Apr 2027.

During gap weeks (e.g. 25 Oct–1 Nov 2026), London and New York were separated by 4 hours instead of 5. A fixed 2:00 PM GMT schedule hit 10:00 AM EDT in summer but 9:00 AM EST in winter. ## How to Find Your Brand's Best Time to Post on LinkedIn for Maximum Engagement

Global benchmarks provide starting parameters, but your unique follower demographics determine your true optimal schedule to discover the **best time to post on LinkedIn for maximum engagement**:

### Step 1: Audit LinkedIn Native Page Analytics
In Creator or Page Analytics, check **Top Locations**. If 60% of followers are in US Eastern and 20% in the UK, schedule between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM EST (1:00 PM–3:00 PM GMT). Review the last 90 days by engagement rate. Because the algorithm prioritizes early comments, post when you can reply actively during the first 60 minutes.

### Step 2: Execute a 4-Week Empirical Split Test
Test your audience across a 4-week plan:
* **Week 1:** Tuesday at 11:00 AM audience local time (Sprout workday window).
* **Week 2:** Wednesday at 4:00 PM audience local time (Buffer top engagement slot).
* **Week 3:** Thursday at 1:00 PM audience local time (post-lunch check-in).
* **Week 4:** Sunday at 4:00 PM audience local time (Vista Social reach window).

Keep format consistent (e.g., PDF carousel). Compare **reach, qualified comments, and reposts**, not likes alone. Genuine peer discussions drive distribution.
