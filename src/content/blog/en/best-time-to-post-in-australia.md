## The Time-Zone Strategy: Converting Australian Slots Globally

Choosing the **best time to post in AEST** requires mapping Australian hours across global financial hubs.

### AEST vs AEDT: Which One Applies?
Sprout's published times are labelled **AEST (Australian Eastern Standard Time, GMT+10)**. Sydney, Melbourne, Canberra, Hobart, and Adelaide run **AEDT (GMT+11)** during daylight saving until **4 April 2027**, reverting to AEST after that. During DST, a 5pm wall-clock post in Sydney is 6am GMT, not 7am; after 4 April 2027 the standard AEST table applies exactly.

### Worked Multi-Zone Example
Facebook's Australian peak **Monday–Friday 4–9pm AEST** equals **6–11am GMT** in London, **1–6am EST** in New York, and **11:30am–4:30pm IST** in Mumbai.

### The Multi-Region Rule: Post Twice, 8 Hours Apart
One post cannot hit the US, UK, and Australia at once: 7pm AEST in Sydney is 9am GMT in London and 4am EST in New York.

**Multi-Region Rule:** Post twice, roughly 8 hours apart:
1.  **Slot 1 (8am AEST / 10pm GMT prev day / 5pm EST prev day):** Reaches the Australian morning commute and US East Coast evening.
2.  **Slot 2 (5pm AEST / 7am GMT / 2am EST):** Captures the Australian evening and UK morning. Plan schedules with our [Meeting Planner](/meeting-planner/).

### The Daylight Saving Time (DST) Trap
Audience daily habits stay fixed to local clock time; what changes is the mathematical offset between zones:
*   **US & Canada:** DST ended Sun 1 Nov 2026; resumes Sun 14 Mar 2027.
*   **UK & Europe:** BST/CEST ended Sun 25 Oct 2026; resumes Sun 28 Mar 2027.
*   **Australia (NSW/VIC/SA/TAS/ACT):** DST began 4 Oct 2026; ends 4 Apr 2027.

Australia's DST runs opposite to the northern hemisphere: Sydney is GMT+11 (AEDT) while New York is on EST (GMT−5), a 16-hour gap. After 14 March 2027 (US moves to EDT), the gap narrows to 15 hours. After 4 April 2027 (Sydney returns to AEST), the gap is 14 hours while New York is on EDT, and 15 hours once New York returns to EST in November 2027. Winter offsets: EST = GMT−5, EDT = GMT−4, PST = GMT−8, CET = GMT+1, IST = GMT+5:30, AEDT = GMT+11, AEST = GMT+10.
